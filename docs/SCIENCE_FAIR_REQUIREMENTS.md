# Science Fair Requirements

## Competition
**REQUIRES VERIFICATION** — The specific science fair competition has not been identified. The student must determine which competition(s) they are entering and obtain the official rules.

## Year
**REQUIRES VERIFICATION** — Competition year must be confirmed.

## Applicable Rules
**REQUIRES VERIFICATION** — Must obtain from competition website:
- ISEF (International Science and Engineering Fair) rules if applicable
- Regional/state fair rules
- Category-specific rules (Environmental Science / Computer Science / Engineering)

## Required Documents (Typical)
| Document | Required? | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Research Plan / Project Plan | Yes | Must be approved before experimentation |
| Abstract (250 words max) | Yes | Submitted with registration |
| Logbook / Research Notebook | Yes | Chronological, handwritten or bound |
| Display Board | Yes | Physical or virtual per competition |
| Safety Forms | Maybe | If human/animal/hazardous materials |
| AI Usage Disclosure | **REQUIRES VERIFICATION** | Many fairs now require this |
| Bibliography / References | Yes | Proper citation format |
| Risk Assessment | Maybe | Depends on project type |

## Display Requirements
**REQUIRES VERIFICATION** — Typical limits:
- Board size: 48" × 36" (tri-fold) or virtual equivalent
- Font sizes: Title ≥ 72pt, headers ≥ 48pt, body ≥ 24pt
- No living organisms, chemicals, sharp objects
- Electrical: battery-powered only or specific outlet rules

## Citation Requirements
**REQUIRES VERIFICATION** — Typically:
- APA, MLA, or IEEE format
- All data sources, papers, tools cited
- In-text citations on board

## AI Requirements
**REQUIRES VERIFICATION** — Increasingly common:
- Disclosure of AI tools used
- Statement of what AI contributed vs. student work
- Some fairs prohibit AI-generated abstracts/display text

## Important Deadlines
**REQUIRES VERIFICATION** — Student must obtain:
- Registration deadline
- Research plan approval deadline
- Abstract submission deadline
- Fair date

## Open Questions for Student
1. Which specific fair(s) are you entering?
2. What category? (Environmental Science, Computer Science, Engineering)
3. What are the exact registration deadlines?
4. Does the fair have an AI usage policy?
4. Are there restrictions on human subjects / environmental sampling?
5. What display format is required (physical board, virtual, both)?
6. Are there page limits for research plan / report?
